Objectives: Connect the PicoScope 4225A safely with the right kit accessories; choose the right PicoScope trigger mode for the event; use Signal and Time rulers, Windowed Zoom, the Waveform Buffer and Reference waveforms; recognize common waveform faults; capture, save and judge a real signal against spec.

PicoScope Capture Workflow read before you start

  1. Look it up first. Find the wiring diagram, connector pin, wire color and expected signal in Service Express / iN. Check the PicoScope Guided tests or Waveform Library for a known-good example.
  2. Connect the scope. Plug the PicoScope 4225A into the laptop by USB, open PicoScope 7 Automotive, and confirm the device is connected. Hang the scope on the S-hook (MI168), clear of moving parts.
  3. Connect the lead. Use the TA404 blue lead on Channel A or the TA405 red lead on Channel B. Each has a permanent 4 mm ground. Clip the ground to battery negative or a clean engine ground with a TA158 battery clip.
  4. Back-pin the signal. Push a TA161/TA162 flexible back-pinning probe into the rear of the plugged-in connector, beside the signal wire. Don't pierce the insulation. Turn on ConnectDetect: the channel LED shows green for a good connection and red for a bad one.
  5. Channel options: choose an input range that keeps the whole signal on screen without "Channel over-range", and DC coupling for most sensor work. PicoBNC+ leads and probes are detected and set up automatically.
  6. Timebase: choose a time/div that shows several cycles at the test RPM. Start from the 20:20 rule (±20 V, 20 ms/div) if you are unsure.
  7. Trigger: Simple Edge, source channel, a threshold inside the signal's range, rising or falling, then the mode: Auto, Repeat or Single.
  8. Capture: click Stopped (or press the space bar) to start running, then create the test condition (key on, crank, idle, snap throttle, wiggle test). Click Running to stop.
  9. Analyze and save: step through the Waveform Buffer, use Windowed Zoom, measure with Signal and Time rulers (values appear in the ruler legend), compare against a Reference waveform, add Notes, and Save as a .psdata file.

Kit items used in this worksheet

  • PQ177 PicoScope 4225A 2-channel standard kit
  • TA404 / TA405 PicoBNC+ test leads, blue (A) / red (B)
  • TA161 / TA162 flexible back-pinning probes
  • TA157 / TA158 battery clips, red / black
  • TA003 / TA004 small crocodile clips
  • MI168 S-hook for hanging the scope
Safety: Route leads away from belts, fans and exhaust. Never connect a test lead directly to ignition secondary voltage; use the TA398 COP probe or TA397 secondary pickup. If PicoScope shows the yellow floating-input warning (more than 30 V between a lead's ground and the scope chassis), stop and recheck your ground connections against the wiring diagram. Follow the PicoScope safety guide.
