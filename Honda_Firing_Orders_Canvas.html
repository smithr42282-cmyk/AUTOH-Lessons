1Background and Finding the Engine Type

When troubleshooting, it is sometimes helpful to know the firing order and the cylinder numbering layout. To use this job aid, you need to know the engine type.

  • Printed service manuals: Go to the General Information section (i), look under Chassis and Paint Codes, and then under Engine Number.
  • Online service information: Enter the keywords ENGINE NUMBER, then select VIN, Engine and Transmission Numbers, and Paint Codes from the list.

The engine type is the first part of the engine number, and its first letter is the engine type letter used below. In the example engine number K24Z2-1000001, the engine type is K24Z2 (a K engine type) and the serial number is 1000001.

3How to Read the Diagrams

  • Each diagram is a top view of the vehicle with the front of the vehicle at the bottom.
  • Cylinder 1 is a white circle with a dark number. All other cylinders are dark circles with white numbers.
  • The gray bar labeled Pulley marks the crankshaft pulley end of the engine.
  • Every diagram is fully described in the text above it, so no information depends on the drawing alone.

4Cylinder Numbering Layouts

Inline 3-Cylinder Engine (L3)

Engine rotation: Clockwise

Firing order: 1, 3, 2

Cylinder layout: Transverse (mounted sideways in the engine bay). Three cylinders sit in one row. Cylinder 1 is at the crankshaft pulley end, followed by 2 and 3. As drawn, the pulley end is on the left.

Inline 4-Cylinder Engines – D and E Engine Types

Applies to: Passport

Engine rotation: Clockwise

Firing order: 1, 3, 4, 2

Cylinder layout: Longitudinal (mounted front to back). Four cylinders sit in one row. Cylinder 1 is at the front of the engine (the crankshaft pulley end, toward the front of the vehicle), followed by 2, 3, and 4 toward the rear.

Inline 4-Cylinder Engines – B, D, and F Engine Types

Engine rotation: Counterclockwise

Firing order: 1, 3, 4, 2

Cylinder layout: Transverse (mounted sideways). Four cylinders sit in one row. Cylinder 1 is at the crankshaft pulley end, followed by 2, 3, and 4. As drawn, the pulley end is on the right, so the cylinders read 4, 3, 2, 1 from left to right.

V6 Engines – C and J Engine Types

Engine rotation: Clockwise

Firing order: 1, 4, 2, 5, 3, 6

Cylinder layout: Transverse (mounted sideways) with two banks of three cylinders. The rear bank (toward the firewall) holds cylinders 1, 2, and 3. The front bank (toward the radiator) holds cylinders 4, 5, and 6. Numbering starts at the crankshaft pulley end, so cylinders 1 and 4 are nearest the pulley. As drawn, the pulley end is on the left.

V6 Engines – V and W Engine Types

Applies to: Passport

Engine rotation: Clockwise

Firing order: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

Cylinder layout: Longitudinal (mounted front to back) with two banks of three cylinders side by side. Odd-numbered cylinders are in one bank: 1, 3, 5 from front to rear (left bank as drawn). Even-numbered cylinders are in the other bank: 2, 4, 6 from front to rear (right bank as drawn). Cylinders 1 and 2 are at the front, at the crankshaft pulley end.
