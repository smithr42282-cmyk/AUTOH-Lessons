Quick Reference read before you start

The screen is a grid (the graticule) of 10 horizontal divisions and 8 vertical divisions. Every reading you take comes from counting divisions and multiplying by the scale setting.

Voltage = vertical divisions × V/div
Time = horizontal divisions × time/div
Frequency (Hz) = 1 ÷ period (s)
Duty cycle (%) = on-time ÷ period × 100

Unit help: 1 ms = 0.001 s, so a 20 ms period → 1 ÷ 0.020 = 50 Hz. Always measure voltage from the ground reference marker (the ◀ on the left edge), not from the bottom of the screen.
