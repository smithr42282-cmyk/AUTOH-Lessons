Quick Reference read before you start

PicoScope 7 draws every waveform on a fixed 10 × 10 grid. The vertical scale is the channel's input range, which spans all 10 vertical divisions, and 0 V sits on the center line. For example, ±10 V covers 20 V over 10 divisions, so each division is 2 V. The horizontal scale is the Timebase, set in time per division across 10 divisions.

V per division = input range ÷ 5 (e.g., ±20 V → 4 V/div)
Voltage = divisions × V/div, or read it from the axis labels
Time = divisions × time/div
Frequency (Hz) = 1 ÷ period (s)
Duty cycle (%) = on-time ÷ period × 100

Where the controls are in PicoScope 7 Automotive

Channel A / B buttonsClick a channel's button to open Channel options: input range (±50 mV to ±200 V), probe, coupling (AC / DC), and Scale & Offset. Channel A traces are blue; Channel B traces are red, matching the TA404 and TA405 test leads.
TimebaseThe button at the top of the screen. Click + / − for more or less time on screen, or click the value to choose a time/div from the list.
TriggerMode: None, Auto, Repeat or Single. Type: Simple Edge. Also Source, Threshold, Direction (rising / falling) and Pre-trigger %. The yellow trigger diamond on the graph can be dragged to change the threshold and pre-trigger point.
Running / StoppedThe green and red buttons at top left, or the space bar, start and stop the capture.
Auto setup · Guided testsAuto setup picks the range, timebase and trigger for you. Guided tests (Automotive menu) load settings and an example waveform for 150+ components. Learn the manual setup first so you can check what they chose.
Over-rangeA red "Channel over-range" indicator means the signal is being clipped. Increase that channel's input range.
Starting point (Pico's "20:20 rule"): when you don't know what a signal looks like, start at ±20 V and 20 ms/div, then refine. Unit help: 1 ms = 0.001 s, so a 20 ms period → 1 ÷ 0.020 = 50 Hz.
