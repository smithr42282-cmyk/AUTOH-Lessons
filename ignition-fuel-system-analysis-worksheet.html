Quick Reference read before you start

Secondary ignition (per cylinder)

  • Firing line: the voltage needed to jump the plug gap (kV). Higher resistance in the gap or the secondary circuit makes it taller.
  • Spark line: the voltage that keeps the spark burning. Its length is the burn time (ms).
  • Coil oscillations: energy left in the coil rings down after the spark ends. Pico's guide looks for at least 4 peaks (upper and lower); fewer points to a coil problem.
  • Dwell / "drop down": a small oscillation in the opposite direction marks the coil's primary switching on to charge for the next spark.
  • Rule of thumb: the coil only has so much energy. A taller firing line usually comes with a shorter burn time.

Injector (saturated, ground-side control)

  • Supply voltage: with the injector off, you see system voltage on the control wire.
  • Turn-on: the ECM driver pulls the circuit to ground and the injector opens.
  • Pulse width: how long the circuit is held at ground (ms). This is the fuel command.
  • Inductive spike: the coil's collapsing field when the driver turns off. A low spike can point to shorted windings.
  • Current ramp: with the TA473 clamp, current rises and shows a small pintle hump as the injector opens.
Honda/Acura note: Most current engines use coil-on-plug coils with the igniter built into the coil, so there's no primary winding terminal to scope. At the coil connector you can scope the ECM's ignition control signal. Look up pins, wire colors and specs in Service Express / iN.

PicoScope 4225A setup for these tests

Secondary (COP)TA398 PicoBNC+ COP & signal probe on Channel A. Clip its ground to a reliable point on the engine block, then hold the paddle flat on top of the coil with the engine running. If the waveform appears upside down, open Channel options → Probes and choose the inverted version of the probe, then capture again. On multi-coil packs, use the TA037 HT extension lead and TA397 secondary pickup.
Injector voltageTA404 lead with a TA161/TA162 back-pinning probe on the injector control (ground-side) wire, and the lead's ground on battery negative. The 4225A's ±200 V input range handles the inductive spike without an attenuator. Start at ±100 V and 1 ms/div.
Injector currentTA473 PicoBNC+ 60 A clamp: select the 20 A range, switch it on, and press zero before clamping around the injector's supply wire. PicoScope detects the clamp and shows amps. A fuse extension lead (TA034 / TA035) can give the clamp access to an injector fuse circuit.
Compare cylindersThe 4225A has 2 channels, so test one or two cylinders at a time. Copy a good cylinder into Reference waveforms and overlay the others against it.
Safety: Never connect a test lead directly to secondary ignition. Use the TA398 COP probe or TA397 pickup only. ConnectDetect does not work with the COP probe or the current clamps because they don't touch the circuit. Keep the scope on the S-hook, and keep hands and leads clear of belts and fans while the engine runs.
