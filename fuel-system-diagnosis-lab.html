Lab-sheet objectives (100% accuracy): use the assigned EWD to explain the fuel circuit to your instructor; complete the theory questions with the EWD, diagnostic scenarios and service manual; measure circuit voltage with the service manual, DVOM, PicoScope and vehicle, and explain how incorrect voltage affects operation; diagnose and repair a fuel system fault with an instructor-approved diagnostic strategy.
Fuel safety: Work in a ventilated area with a fire extinguisher nearby and no sparks or open flame. Relieve fuel pressure exactly as Service Express describes before you open any fuel line. Wear eye protection, catch fuel in an approved container, and wipe up spills immediately.
